# Vanguard Motorcycles 🏍️
### *Born of Iron. Crafted for the Pure Ride.*

A modern, high-performance, lightweight showcase website for **Vanguard Motorcycles** inspired by iconic heritage and performance motorcycle manufacturers such as **Royal Enfield** and **TVS Motor**.

Built with pure semantic HTML5, modern vanilla CSS, and vanilla JavaScript—delivering blazing fast 100/100 Lighthouse performance without heavy build dependencies.

---

## 🌟 Key Features & 10 Cohesive Pages

1. **Home (`index.html`)**
   - Fullscreen cinematic hero banner with flagship twin-cylinder motorcycle
   - **Native Web Audio Exhaust Sound Synthesizer**: Hear the authentic 270° parallel-twin engine thumping directly in your browser without loading heavy audio files!
   - Fleet preview cards with quick technical specs
   - Interactive On-Road EMI Financing Estimator with live recalculation
   - Community Himalayan Odyssey spotlight & newsletter brochure lead capture

2. **Motorcycles Fleet (`motorcycles.html`)**
   - Category filters: Modern Classic, Adventure, Cruiser, Scrambler
   - Side-by-side technical specification comparison table
   - Direct booking & brochure anchors

3. **Flagship Deep-Dive: Hunter 650 Twin (`model-detail.html`)**
   - **Interactive Live Colorway Switcher** (British Racing Emerald, Obsidian Chrome, Sunset Amber, Gunmetal Stealth)
   - Expandable technical specifications accordion (Engine, Chassis, Dimensions, Electricals)
   - Sticky quick reservation bar

4. **Book a Test Ride (`book-test-ride.html`)**
   - High-conversion 4-step booking workflow (Motorcycle &rarr; Showroom &rarr; Date & Window &rarr; Rider Credentials)
   - Real-time booking summary card
   - Immediate confirmation reference generation (e.g., `VG-TR-88219`)

5. **Showroom Locator (`dealers.html`)**
   - Search & city filters for authorized dealerships
   - Facility badges, showroom hours, and navigation prompts
   - **Authorized Dealership Franchise Application** lead form

6. **"Make It Yours" Customizer (`configurator.html`)**
   - Interactive 3D builder: Select base motorcycle, exhaust system, and touring accessories
   - **Real-Time Dynamic Price Calculator** updating as you add or remove factory parts
   - Custom build quotation lead request form

7. **Rider Apparel & Genuine Accessories (`apparel-accessories.html`)**
   - CE-certified leather riding jackets, helmets, gloves, and touring luggage
   - Showroom reservation lead modal with size selection

8. **Rides & Community (`rides-community.html`)**
   - Trans-Himalayan Odyssey, Konkan Coastal Thunder, and Sunday Breakfast Rides
   - Interactive Ride Registration lead form

9. **Service & 24x7 Roadside Assistance (`service.html`)**
   - Periodic maintenance booking with doorstep pickup toggle
   - **Emergency 24x7 RSA SOS Request Form** with rapid dispatch simulator

10. **Heritage & Company (`about.html`)**
    - Brand lineage timeline from 1948 Redditch to the 2026 Twin-Cylinder Renaissance
    - Core engineering philosophy and corporate contact inquiry form

## 📋 Real-Time Lead Management (Live Demo Review)

All forms across the site are fully connected to a centralized lead storage engine powered by `localStorage`.
- Click the **"Leads Admin"** floating pill in the bottom-right corner of any page.
- View captured leads in real time (Test rides, finance requests, custom quotes, franchise applications, service bookings).
- Export captured leads to a formatted **CSV file** or test instant lead submissions.

## 🚀 Deployment

Optimized for instant zero-config deployment on **Vercel** with `vercel.json` included:
```bash
npx vercel
```
Or open any page directly in any modern browser without needing a build step.
