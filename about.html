Historical Lineage

Seven Decades Of Speed & Soul

1948

The Foundry in Redditch

Vanguard begins production of cast-iron single cylinder 350cc roadsters, renowned for unbreakable endurance across post-war Britain.

1964

Isle of Man TT Podium

The Vanguard 500 GP racer averages 102.4 MPH on the Snaefell Mountain Course, cementing our reputation for high-speed stability and frame balance.

2008

Global Tech Centre at Bruntingthorpe

Establishment of our state-of-the-art engineering center combining British chassis design with high-volume precision manufacturing in India.

2026

The Twin-Cylinder Renaissance

Launch of the acclaimed 648cc parallel-twin engine platform, winning global motorcycle of the year honours in 14 countries.
