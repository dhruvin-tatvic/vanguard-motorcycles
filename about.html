Heritage

Seven Decades of Motorcycling

1948

The Redditch Workshop

Vanguard begins production of cast-iron single cylinder roadsters renowned for endurance and simplicity.

1964

Isle of Man Podium

The Vanguard 500 GP racer achieves podium speeds on the Mountain Course, cementing our chassis pedigree.

2008

Global Tech Centre

Establishment of modern engineering facilities combining British chassis design with high-volume precision manufacturing in India.

2026

The Twin-Cylinder Platform

Launch of the acclaimed 648cc parallel-twin engine platform across cafe racer, cruiser, and scrambler models.
